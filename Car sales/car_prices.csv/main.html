📖 Understanding R² Score

What is R²? Think of R² as a report card for our model. A score of 0.87 means we can predict 87% of car price changes correctly using just odometer reading, condition, body type, and model name.

Why this matters: The model scored 98% on training data and 87% on new cars it had never seen. This gap is small and healthy. It shows that the model learned pricing patterns rather than just memorizing examples. Sales managers can trust these predictions in the real world.

🎯 Simple Pricing Formulas

Quick price calculator: The formulas for popular models so sales teams can estimate prices instantly without a computer. Just plug in the odometer and condition rating.

Nissan Altima: Price = $13,457 + $0.07 × Odometer + $67.84 × Condition

Explaination: Start with $13,457. plus 7 cents for every kilometer driven. Add $68 for each point of condition rating. (Accuracy: 74%)

Toyota Camry: Price = $13,066 + $0.07 × Odometer + $76.07 × Condition + $226 × Sedan

Explaination: Works like Altima but condition matters more at $76 per point. Customers love Camry sedans, so add $226 bonus if it's a sedan. (Accuracy: 82% ✨ Most reliable!)

Ford F-150: Price = $24,542 + $0.12 × Odometer + $94.60 × Condition

Explaination: Trucks start expensive at $24,542. They lose more value per kilometer (12 cents) because work trucks get heavy use. Condition is king at $95 per rating point. (Accuracy: 57%)

💡 Real Example

Let's price a Camry: A Camry sedan with 50,000 km and condition rating 4.5. The calculation: $13,066 + (0.07 × 50,000) + (76.07 × 4.5) + $226 = $10,134. The sales team has instant pricing power during customer negotiations.
